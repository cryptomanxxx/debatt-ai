# Swedish narration credits

These three short demonstration voice clips were generated locally with
[Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) using the open Swedish model
[`sv_SE-nst-medium`](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/sv/sv_SE/nst/medium).

The model was trained by KBLab at the National Library of Sweden. Its model
card states that the training dataset is CC0 licensed. The Piper repository
and the `piper-voices` repository are MIT licensed.

This is a sample Swedish synthetic voice, **not** a recreation or clone of
the original Professor Oraklet / ResponsiveVoice voice on Debatt-AI. These
audio files are demos; no AI model is called when a visitor plays them.
